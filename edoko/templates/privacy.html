{{ _("Last changed: 2026-11-01") }}

{% trans %}e-DoKo is a small site to play Doppelkopf with friends. It is run by Arun Persaud as a hobby. This page says what the site stores about you, who else gets to see it, and how to get rid of it.{% endtrans %}

{{ _("What is stored") }}

  • {% trans %}Your account: the name you play under, your email address, your time zone and language, and your settings (how you want to get mail, your picture).{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Your games: which tables and games you played in, the cards, the moves, the results, and the remarks you wrote in a game. Other players of a game see what you played and wrote there. Your cards in a game that is not over are never shown to anybody else.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Private notes you write on a game are only shown to you.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Passkeys: the public part of each passkey, its name, and when it was last used. The private part never leaves your device.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Login links by mail: only a scrambled form (a hash) of the link is kept, until it has been used or has expired.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Sign-up and invitations: the email address and a scrambled form (a hash) of the link are kept until the link has been used, and at most two days after it has expired. Nothing else is stored until you have set up your passkey and your account exists.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Going on at a table: who proposed more rounds, and who agreed or declined. Players only see who proposed; nobody is told who declined.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Live mode: while you have switched it on for a game, the time of the last check of the page is kept (and when live mode runs out). The other players of that game see a dot next to your name while it is on. Nothing else about what you do on the page is stored.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Your picture: a generated one, or the one you uploaded, or a copy of your Gravatar if you asked for it. You can delete the copies in the settings.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}Mails that could not be delivered: if a mail to your address bounces or is reported as unwanted, the address and the reason are noted, and no more mail is sent until it is fixed.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}To limit how often login and sign-up links can be asked for, a scrambled form of the address asked for, and a keyed hash of your network address (which cannot be turned back into the address), are kept for one day.{% endtrans %}

{{ _("Logs") }}

{% trans %}The web server writes an access log: the network address of the visitor, the page asked for, the answer and the browser name. The secret links to a seat in a game (the links in the mails) are removed from this log. The log is used to find errors and abuse.{% endtrans %}

{{ _("Cookies") }}

  • {% trans %}A session cookie that keeps you logged in and protects forms against forgery. It is only set when you log in or open a page with a form.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}A cookie that remembers for 30 days that you did not want to create a passkey.{% endtrans %}
  • {% trans %}A cookie that remembers for a year the language you picked at the top of the page, if you were not logged in. It is only set when you click the language.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}There is no tracking, no advertising, no analytics, and the pages load nothing from other sites (no fonts, scripts or pictures).{% endtrans %}

{{ _("Who else is involved") }}

  • Cloudflare: {% trans %}the site is reached through Cloudflare (name service, protection against abuse, and the connection to the server). Cloudflare sees the traffic to the site.{% endtrans %}
  • Amazon Web Services (SES): {% trans %}the mails (invitations, login links, "it is your turn", the digest) are sent with Amazon SES. Amazon gets your address and the text of the mail.{% endtrans %}
  • Gravatar: {% trans %}only if you press the button to fetch your Gravatar, the server asks gravatar.com for the picture of your address (a hash of the address is sent) and stores a copy of the picture here, on our server. Your browser never contacts Gravatar, and the copy can be deleted in the settings.{% endtrans %}

{% trans %}Your data is not sold and not given to anybody else.{% endtrans %}
