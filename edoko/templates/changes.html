2026-11-03

  • Live mode for playing in real time: a “Live” button on the game page. While it is on, the page looks for new moves every 10 seconds and you get no “your turn” mail for that game. A green dot next to a name shows who else is live. It is off again whenever you open the page anew. If you leave, or it is your turn and you do not move within 5 minutes, the mail comes as usual; live mode also ends by itself after 30 minutes without a move.
  • Language: the language in your settings now applies to the pages too (before, only the mails followed it). Visitors who are not logged in can switch between EN and DE at the top of the page.
  • The game page: bigger cards on the table and in your own hand, no play-order numbers on the cards (the arrow shows the order), and no “on turn” text (the outline shows it). The comment box says “Add a comment” and “Send”.
  • After someone has reminded a player, the game page says so and offers no second reminder for a week. Cancelling an idle game asks first, with a warning, and is not possible for 3 days after a reminder.
  • Fixed: the standings and summary of the old tables showed 0 points for everybody; the points of all old games are filled in. A comment saved unchanged is no longer marked “edited”. German wording: “Karten” instead of “Blätter”.

2026-11-01

  • A start page for newcomers, and sign-up with a passkey. Admins can close sign-up or make it invitation-only.
  • A finished table shows a summary: points, games won, solos, foxes caught and a game-by-game table.
  • What was said before the game is shown as “Before the game”, next to the trick numbers.
  • The remark box is below your own cards, with a hint to write the remark before clicking the card.
  • Umlauts in old comments are repaired.

2026-10-31

  • Playing on the new site: join a game, declare a solo, wedding, poverty or nines, and play your cards, with all the rules of the old site (calls, Schweinchen, dullen).
  • Tables: start a table with house rules and an optional number of games; keep playing with the seats rotating; standings and a winner at the end.
  • Mail: choose normal, fewer or no mails, or a digest at the hours you like; your time zone and language; reminders for games that wait for a week, and cancelling after a month.
  • Remarks with a card: on any card of the trick shown, editable for 15 minutes.
  • A privacy page that says what is stored.
